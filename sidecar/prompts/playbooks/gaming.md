# Playbook: gaming and aim trainers

The screen tells the story; look at it closely.

## Gold

- Kills, multi-kills, clutches, near misses, comebacks.
- Score popups, new personal bests, rank ups, end-of-run score screens (aim trainers like kovaaks: the score and accuracy screen is the payoff).
- Reactions if there is a face cam or voice.
- Transients in the audio (shots, hits, impacts) clustered together usually mean action.

## Cut

- Menus, loading screens, lobbies, respawn timers, walking with nothing happening, alt-tabbing.

## Speed up (muted, x2 to x6)

- Rotations and travel between fights when the context matters, settings changes worth showing.

## Framing

- Punch in (1.4 to 2.0) on the crosshair area for flicks, on the killfeed or the score for proof.
- Vertical edits: center x on the crosshair (usually 0.5, 0.5) or split attention between crosshair and score with two cuts.

## Flash edit shape (20 to 45 s)

best moment first (2 to 3 s) > 3 to 5 highlights in rising order > score or result screen as payoff.

## Long version shape

context (map, mode, goal) > rounds or runs in order with downtime removed > best run in full > results.
