# Playbook: podcast and interview

Speech carries everything. Read the transcript before watching.

## Gold

- Self-contained stories, strong opinions, surprising facts, funny exchanges, quotable lines.
- A clear question and its answer.
- Reactions that land: laughter, disbelief, "wait, what".

## Cut

- Retakes, false starts, "let's cut that", technical chatter, long tangents unless asked.
- Never cut inside a sentence, never keep an answer without its question.
- Never speed up speech.

## Framing

- Alternate punch-ins (1.0 then 1.2 to 1.3 on the speaker) every few sentences to hide cuts and stress punchlines.
- Vertical edits: zoom x on the person speaking; with two people in one wide shot, switch x between them with the dialogue.

## Captions

Mandatory for shorts: 2 to 5 words per caption, synced to the speech, emphasis words kept.

## Flash edit shape (30 to 90 s)

the punchline or the boldest line first (as a flash forward) > the setup > the full story > a clean ending line.

## Long version shape

Full conversation in order, retakes and dead air removed, chapters on topic changes.
