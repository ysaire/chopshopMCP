# Playbook: unboxing, desk setup, keyboard and asmr

No voice. The sound is the content, the hands tell the story.

## Gold (keep real time, full sound)

- First reveal: lid lift, product sliding out, first full view.
- Satisfying sounds: switch clicks and thocks, keycap pulls and presses, tape peel, foam and paper rustle, magnets, cable clicks.
- Typing test or sound test: usually the payoff, often the best hook too.
- Light moments: rgb turning on, underglow, screen waking up.
- Macro textures: knurling, logos, engravings, keycap legends.

## Cut

- Hands out of frame, repositioning the camera, reaching for tools, waiting.
- Repeated identical actions: keep two or three, montage the rest.
- Fumbles, dropped parts, unless they are funny.

## Speed up (muted, x4 to x8)

- Long builds: screwing plates, installing switches one by one, lubing, cable management.

## Framing

- Punch in (1.3 to 1.6) on the hands and the detail that makes the sound.
- Vertical edits: the product usually sits in a known spot of the desk, set zoom x/y on it rather than the frame center.

## Flash edit shape (30 to 60 s)

hook (sound test or reveal, 2 s) > box and first look (3 to 5 cuts) > build timelapse (3 to 6 s) > details punch-ins > sound test payoff.

## Long version shape

arrival and box > unboxing in order > contents laid out > build (timelapses on the repetitive parts) > close-ups > sound test > final glamour shots.
